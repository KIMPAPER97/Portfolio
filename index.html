01 — About

Designing matter
at the atomic scale.

I'm Jonghwi Kim, a student at Korea Science Academy of KAIST with a deep passion for understanding and engineering materials from the ground up — atom by atom, electron by electron.

My work spans molecular dynamics simulations using LAMMPS with potentials ranging from Lennard-Jones to machine-learned force fields, electrochemical nanostructure synthesis through cyclic voltammetry and electrodeposition, and quantum chemical calculations with Gaussian, ORCA, and Quantum Espresso.

I also build custom tools — from Python finite-difference solvers for Butler-Volmer kinetics to Blender plugins for 3D trajectory visualization and interactive web applications for science communication.

03 — Projects

Selected work.

A curated selection of research projects, simulations, and tools I've built.

Featured — R&E Project

Cu₂O Nanoparticle Electrodeposition Simulation

Comprehensive computational study of cuprous oxide electrodeposition from CuSO₄/NaCl electrolytes. Integrates finite-difference diffusion solvers for Fick's Second Law, Butler-Volmer electrode kinetics, Scharifker-Hills nucleation theory, and Langmuir adsorption. Simulates a three-electrode cell with 5 mM CuSO₄ at 328 K (55 °C), pH 9.0, 50 mV/s scan rate across 5 CV cycles.

Python LAMMPS Butler-Volmer Fick's Law NumPy Matplotlib
Cu²⁺ + e⁻ → Cu⁺  |  2Cu⁺ + H₂O → Cu₂O + 2H⁺
📖

Comprehensive Review of MD Potentials

Academic paper surveying the evolution from Lennard-Jones pairwise interactions to modern machine-learned force fields (DeepMD, neural network potentials). Covers CHARMM, AMBER, OPLS, ReaxFF, AMOEBA, and PCFF with trade-off analysis of accuracy vs. computational cost.

LaTeX LAMMPS Python agents4science 2025
💧

Microplastics Removal via Chitosan Flocculation

IChTO Problem 11 — Designed a chitosan coagulation-flocculation system to reduce micro- and nanoplastics in seaweed soup. Modeled polyethylene-chitosan interactions with LAMMPS molecular dynamics and quantified aggregation kinetics.

LAMMPS LaTeX Beamer Coarse-Grained MD
🍪

Restoring Crispiness in Soggy Chips

IChTO Problem 12 — Ethanol-based moisture displacement from starch matrix. Coarse-grained LAMMPS simulation modeling water displacement: ethanol-water interactions outcompete starch-water bonds, drawing moisture out and restoring the glassy crispy state.

LAMMPS Python RDF Analysis MSD
🔥

Amorphous SiO₂ Thermal Dynamics

Molecular dynamics simulations of amorphous silica using BKS potentials (Buckingham + Coulomb). Temperature ramping from 300 K to 2000 K with NVT ensemble, studying thermal expansion, structural transitions, and radial distribution functions.

LAMMPS BKS Potential Python OVITO
📦

LAMMPS → Blender 3D Visualization

Custom Python plugin for Blender 4.x that imports LAMMPS trajectory dump files and converts atomic coordinates into animated 3D spheres with automatic material assignment per atom type. Supports large trajectory files for cinematic renders.

Blender Python LAMMPS
📈

Iron Electroanalysis via Cyclic Voltammetry

Experimental electrochemistry project — quantified iron concentrations using CV with 6-point calibration (10 mM standard at variable dilutions). Analyzed oxidation/reduction peaks across 4 unknown samples with method validation per SOP.

CV Model 700E Calibration Excel
🌡️

Korean Sea Temperature Trend Analysis

Comparative analysis of vertical water temperature profiles across East Sea, South Sea, and West Sea (2012 vs. 2024). Python-based visualization of depth-dependent stratification, seasonal variation (6.86–29.8 °C), and long-term climate trends.

Python Matplotlib Climate Data
🎓

Graduation Research — Nano-Biosensors

Capstone project focused on electrochemical nano-roughening and gold nanostructure synthesis for biosensor applications. Investigating nanoporous gold electrodes, aptamer-based detection, and surface area enhancement through controlled electrodeposition.

Electrochemistry Gold Nanostructures Biosensors
💓

Forensic Heart Rate Simulation

Real-time heart rate monitor simulation with dual-transition BPM modeling — resting to excited to flatline. Features Gaussian noise with configurable σ for realistic physiological variation and terminal-style visualization.

Python NumPy Matplotlib
